# Motion, Audio, And Haptics

## Motion
- Normal land: quick settle, restrained bounce
- Perfect land: 120-180 ms micro slow-motion, magnetic snap, tower pulse
- Slice miss: graceful trimmed overhang drift with no chaotic physics
- Failure: tasteful pause before results

## Audio
- Normal: clean understated tok
- Perfect: brighter tick with slight pitch lift
- Streaks: harmonic reinforcement that stays premium, not loud
- Slice: muted shhk
- Fail/new best: soft signature punctuation

## Haptics
- Normal: light impact
- Perfect: stronger refined transient
- Fail: subdued but noticeable confirmation
- New best: warmer celebratory pattern

## Accessibility
- Reduced motion removes nonessential drift, large pulses, and slow-motion
- Mute and haptics toggles always override feedback systems
